Support

Flamboyance — a quiz game about English collective nouns for animals.

How do I change my display name?

Open the You tab (bottom right), type a new name, and tap Save. This is the name other players see on the league leaderboard and in duels or parties.

How does the weekly League work?

Every player who's ever played the app is on one global weekly leaderboard. Duel scores always count. Speed Run counts your best run of the day (not every run). Study counts a running daily total, capped, so it rewards a solid session without letting repetition dominate the board. It resets at the start of each week.

I found a wrong or missing collective noun

We'd genuinely like to know — email us below with the noun/animal pair and what you think is wrong.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the You tab and tap Delete my data. This is immediate and permanent — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what's removed.

A Duel or Party isn't connecting

Duel matchmaking needs another player searching at the same time, so a wait is normal if the app doesn't have many concurrent players yet. For Party, double check the room code was typed correctly — codes are case-insensitive but must match exactly otherwise.
