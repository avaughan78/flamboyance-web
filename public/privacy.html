Privacy Policy

Flamboyance · Last updated 17 August 2026

Flamboyance is a quiz game about English collective nouns for animals. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and how to remove it.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account ID. The app signs you in anonymously the first time you open it — no email, phone number, or password. This ID lets your progress, scores, and league standing persist between sessions.
  • A display name. You choose this yourself in the "You" tab. It's shown to other players on the league leaderboard and in duels/parties. It defaults to "Player" until you change it.
  • Gameplay data. Quiz answers, duel results, speed run scores, study progress, and league points — all tied to your anonymous account ID so the app can show your stats and standings.

What we don't collect

No real name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifiers, or browsing history. Flamboyance shows no ads and does no third-party tracking or analytics.

How data is used

Solely to run the app's own features: your scores, your league rank, and what other players see of you in multiplayer modes. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to track you across other apps or websites.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our backend provider, secured in transit with HTTPS and protected by row-level database policies so you can only read or change your own data (other players' data is visible only where the game itself shows it, like the league leaderboard).

Deleting your data

Open the You tab in the app and tap Delete my data. This permanently removes your account, display name, scores, ratings, league points, and history — immediately, with no need to contact us.

Children

Flamboyance doesn't knowingly collect data from children and contains no age-gated content — it's a general-audience word game.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too.
