Delete your data

Flamboyance · Last updated 25 August 2026

You can permanently delete the data associated with your Flamboyance profile. This includes your display name, ratings, league points, scores, game history, and study progress.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Flamboyance.
  2. Open the You tab.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Tap Delete my data, then confirm.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Can't access the app?

If you have uninstalled Flamboyance or cannot access the app, email avaughan78@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Flamboyance data”. Please include the display name used in the app so we can locate the correct anonymous profile.
